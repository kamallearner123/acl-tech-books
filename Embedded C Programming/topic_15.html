Topic 15

Naming Conventions

Names are part of the interface. Use nouns for objects and verbs for actions, include units such as timeout_ms and voltage_mv, avoid unexplained abbreviations, and use consistent casing for types, functions, constants, and macros.

Good names reduce review time and expose assumptions before code executes.
