Standards, Protocols & Datasheets Compendium

Writing safety-critical Embedded C requires adhering to international regulatory frameworks and standard communication bus protocols. This compendium summarizes the governing functional safety standards, hardware communication physical layers, and semiconductor documentation.

5.1 International Functional Safety Standards

Standard Industry Domain Safety Integrity Classification Core Firmware Mandates
ISO 26262 Road Vehicles (Automotive) ASIL A, B, C, D (D = Highest) Strict MISRA-C compliance, 100% MC/DC test coverage, hardware lockstep, safe state transitions.
IEC 62304 Medical Device Software Class A (No injury), B (Non-serious), C (Death/Serious injury) Static memory allocation (no heap), strict requirement-to-test traceability, defensive architecture.
IEC 61508 Industrial Functional Safety SIL 1, SIL 2, SIL 3, SIL 4 Dual-channel redundancy (1oo2 / 2oo3), diagnostic coverage >99%, hardware watchdog timer supervision.
DO-178C Commercial & Military Aviation DAL A (Catastrophic) down to DAL E (No effect) Exhaustive requirements verification, zero dead code, object-code to source-code traceability for Level A.
IEEE 1547 / UL 1741 Solar & Grid Energy Inverters Category I, II, III (Grid ride-through) Anti-islanding disconnection in <100 ms, frequency and voltage disturbance trip curves.

5.2 Embedded Communication Bus Protocol Comparison

Bus Protocol Physical Layer & Wiring Maximum Throughput Maximum Reach Key Strengths & Primary Use Case
SPI (Serial Peripheral Interface) 4-Wire synchronous (SCK, MOSI, MISO, CS#) 10 MHz – 80+ MHz < 30 cm (On-board PCB) Ultra-fast full-duplex streaming with zero protocol overhead; perfect for IMUs and Flash memory.
I2C / I3C 2-Wire open-drain with pull-ups (SCL, SDA) 100 kHz (Std), 400 kHz (Fast), 12.5 MHz (I3C) < 50 cm (On-board PCB) Multi-device addressing on 2 shared pins; standard for temperature, barometers, and EEPROMs.
CAN 2.0B / CAN-FD Differential twisted-pair (CAN_H, CAN_L, 120Ω termination) 1.0 Mbps (CAN 2.0B), 5.0–8.0 Mbps (CAN-FD) 40 meters (at 1 Mbps) to 1,000 m (at 50 kbps) Hardware arbitration, differential noise rejection, CRC check; universal automotive standard.
Automotive Ethernet Single unshielded twisted-pair (100BASE-T1 / 1000BASE-T1) 100 Mbps – 1.0 Gbps 15 meters High-bandwidth point-to-point IP data for ADAS radar, cameras, and domain controllers.
RS-485 / Modbus Differential pair (A, B) with multi-drop transceivers 10 Mbps (short distance) or 115.2 kbps Up to 1,200 meters Immunity to industrial ground loops and electrical factory noise; standard for PLCs and smart meters.
