Topic 24

Application, Driver, HAL, and BSP Layers

Application: product behavior
Services and state machines
Driver: peripheral behavior
HAL: stable hardware interface
BSP: board pins, clocks, and startup

The application owns product policy. Drivers own peripheral access. The HAL defines portable operations. The BSP binds those operations to a board. Separation of concerns makes hardware replacement and host testing practical.
